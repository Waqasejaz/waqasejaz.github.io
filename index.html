01 / AboutBehind the research

Researching the space between information and action.

I am a Research Associate at the Reuters Institute for the Study of Journalism, University of Oxford. My work explores how people encounter, interpret and respond to information about consequential issues facing contemporary societies.

Drawing on political communication, journalism studies and psychology, I study climate change communication, news consumption, misinformation, trust, political identities and public opinion.

My research has a comparative focus, bringing together evidence from countries in the Global North and South. I am particularly interested in how social and political contexts shape people's understanding of public issues.

Before joining Oxford, I was an Assistant Professor at the National University of Sciences and Technology in Pakistan. I completed my PhD in Media and Communication Science at the Technical University of Ilmenau, Germany.
